# ALMA Lifestyle Brand Guidelines

## Business Model

ALMA Lifestyle is a curated ecommerce destination that sources premium products from:

- Own manufacturing (factory-made)
- Trusted vendors (wholesale)
- Quality resellers

## Brand Voice

- **NOT:** Artisanal, handmade, traditional weaving
- **YES:** Premium, curated, modern, elegant, affordable luxury

## Forbidden Terms (NEVER use)

- handmade / handcrafted
- we make / we craft
- our artisans / our weavers
- হাতে বানানো / হাতে তৈরি / তাঁতিদের
- কারিগরের তৈরি

## Approved Terms

- premium quality / carefully curated
- we curate / we source / we select
- trusted partners / quality makers
- প্রিমিয়াম মান / যত্নে বাছাই / বিশ্বস্ত

## Brand Story

ALMA Lifestyle was created to bring elegance, comfort, and modern lifestyle products together in one trusted destination. We believe everyone deserves premium style and quality without paying luxury-level prices.

## USPs (Top 3)

1. Premium Quality Products with Elegant & Trendy Designs
2. Trusted Customer Service with Fast & Reliable Delivery
3. Affordable Luxury — High-End Lifestyle Products at the Best Price
